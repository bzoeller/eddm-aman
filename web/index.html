      As APP: drag a label to swap or onto the other runway, drag it into the hold list to take it out. Click a label for CHANGE RWY, HOLD and RECOMPUTE. Drag aircraft from the hold list straight back onto a runway. Click the RWY box for runway change (now or at a time) and runway closures; a planned change shows as a green dashed line on the timeline. UNDO (Ctrl+Z) reverts the last action. The display updates every 5 seconds. Click "static" to freeze the sequence. FLOW at the bottom sets the spacing per runway.
